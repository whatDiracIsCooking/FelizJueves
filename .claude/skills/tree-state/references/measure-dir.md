# `measure/`

Moved verbatim from `.claude/CLAUDE.md`, "The state of the tree, its table" (indexed by `../SKILL.md`).

**live, never a gate.** pytest as a harness, outside `testpaths` (so no bare `pytest`, fast tier or kernel tier collects it), its files `measure_*.py`, its `conftest.py` loading `test/conftest.py`'s fixtures and card lock and putting `test/` on `sys.path`. `devtools/measure.sh list` names the sweeps: the timed ones (`wallclock`, `wallclock-k`, `wallclock-f`, `levers`, `builds`, `sort-key`, `roofline`, `ceilings`, `kblock`, `scheme-ab`, `kblock-rolled`, `kblock-rolled-profile`, `kblock-transpose`, `kblock-transpose-profile`, `kblock-column`, `kblock-column-profile`, `df-lowstack`; alone, no xdist, behind the gate, writing their stores, which default to the tracked files in `devtools/config.sh`), `scatter-tail` and `probes` (the ncu/rocprofv3 counter witnesses and the probe's published-peak bound), `kblock-split`, `kblock-rolled-compile`, `kblock-column-compile`, `df-lowstack-compile`, `df-grad-lowstack-compile`, `d2-df-lowstack-compile` (#694, `sm_86` rows only so far), `g1e-compile`, `gjab-compile` and `gk-compile` (compiler readings, no card), `registry-cost` (#652: every registry case's compile and `gpu` cost per backend, measured from `test/compile-only-timings.xml` and any kernel-tier `pytest.xml` reports, else fitted on L per ladder, family or all, into `data/measured/registry_cost.json`; no compiler, no card), `recheck` (recompile and compare against `kernels.json` / `fock_baseline.json`; a compiler, no card) and `check` (the tracked stores and reports recomputed, ~20 s, no compiler and no card), plus `remeasure` (`python -m felizjueves.performance.measured --remeasure`). A kernel-text change that moves a stored `source_sha256` fails `check`, not the PR gate: re-measure, then `check`. `test_claims.py` scans both trees for `claim` marks.
